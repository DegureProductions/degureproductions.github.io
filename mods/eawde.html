What is Equestria at War: Definitive Edition?

Equestria at War: Definitive Edition is an adaptation of HOI4's Equestria at War to the game Age of History 2: Definitive Edition. The objective of the mod is mainly HOI4 accuracy, with lots of adapted events/focuses. There will be a classical AoH2 version with Loji dex, for the players whose devices lag with DE.

What is already finished of the project?

As of now, the civilizations and ideologies are finished. The main developer is working on a new map as of now.

Where can I download it?

You can download it here! But if you want to see updates, join the server in the mods page.
